# Bagwise

## Why

Bags fill up fast in WoW Forever and most of what sits in them you kept because you were not sure. Quest items with no quest in the log. Meat that might feed a recipe. A soulbound item locked to the wrong class. A reagent for a profession you do not have. Each one is small, none of it is grey, so the usual junk addons do not touch it and you do not either. Bagwise looks at these items and puts a small dot on the ones with a clear answer. Green means safe to delete. Yellow means worth a glance. No dot means it does not know. You still decide what to delete, with better information to go on.

## What it is Not

- Not a bag replacement. The default Blizzard bag stays; the addon just overlays a small corner dot.
- Not a deletion tool. No delete button, no auto-destroy, no popups. Every deletion is still your choice.
- Not a gear or profession evaluator. It only flags items that can't or won't be used. It does not grade equippable gear or weigh offspec choices.
